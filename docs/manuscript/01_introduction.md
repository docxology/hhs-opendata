# Introduction

The HHS Medicaid Provider Spending publication reports aggregated use
and payments by billing provider, servicing provider, procedure code
and month [@hhs_spending]. The public dataset supports descriptive
analysis of services represented in the release and the payments
recorded for those services.

The repository's analysis reports time trends, procedure summaries,
and statistical signals for review. The evidence in this note comes
from the archived annual and exploratory summaries. They expose a
small reproducible subset of the full analytical results.
